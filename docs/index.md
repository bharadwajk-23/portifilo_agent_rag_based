# Project Index — Youngsoft India

This document contains the complete list of projects built by **Youngsoft India (Youngsoft-India)**, covering healthcare, artificial intelligence, clinical documentation, conversational AI, healthcare reporting, digital prescriptions, physiotherapy, recruitment, candidate assessment, health insurance, and insurance chatbots. The documented projects are **Scriber Agent** (`scriber_agent.md`), an AI-powered healthcare transcription and clinical documentation platform supporting real-time medical transcription, clinical documentation, and AI-generated summaries; **AI Listener** (`ai_listener.md`), a healthcare conversational AI platform for patient interactions, voice-based conversations, and clinical summaries; **Intelligent Reporting System** (`intelligent-reporting-system.md`), a natural-language healthcare data querying and reporting platform; **Digital Prescription & Virtual Assistant** (`digital-prescription-virtual-assistant-platform.md`), a healthcare platform for digital prescriptions, medication management, and virtual healthcare assistance; **AI Physiotherapy Call Agent** (`retell_caller_agent.md`), an AI voice agent for physiotherapy patient communication, appointments, follow-ups, and patient engagement; **YSIRas** (`ysi_ras.md`), an AI-powered recruitment and candidate assessment platform for resume screening, interviews, recruitment workflows, and candidate evaluation; and **MyTruAdvantage Health Plan Chatbot** (`siho_rag.md`), an AI-powered health insurance chatbot for health plans, benefits, healthcare providers, pharmacies, and insurance-related queries.
